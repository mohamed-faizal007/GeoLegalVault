First, login and roles. There are five roles: Administrator, Legal Officer, Reviewing Officer, Authorized Staff and Auditor. A role is a set of permissions. Here Authorized Staff is signed in. The menu has Upload, but no Admin Panel and no Audit Logs. Next, a Legal Officer signs in, in a completely separate browser session. Then an Administrator. Their menu shows the Admin Panel and Audit Logs, but no Upload. Administrators manage users and zones, but they cannot upload, review or approve. That separation of duties is deliberate. Each user also has a clearance level: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted or Top Secret. Clearance decides which documents a person may see. The menu is only a convenience. The server checks role and clearance on every single request.
