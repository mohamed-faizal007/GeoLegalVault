To summarise the parts. The front end is React. The back end is FastAPI in Python. MongoDB stores the data. RustFS in development, and Cloudflare R2 in production, store the files. web3.py talks to a Solidity smart contract on the Sepolia test network. Docker runs the services, and a CI pipeline runs the tests. Future work includes the open items in the project's own tracker: stronger login and secret handling, deployment hardening, a hash chain for the audit log, and malware scanning of uploads. There is also a planned next version that reduces how much you must trust the single service wallet. I won't describe its design here.
