Finally, where everything lives. The Auditor opens the Audit Logs page. Every action is a row: who, what, to what, and the result. Rows are only ever added. In the first supplementary view, these are the audit rows for our lease: upload, submit, review, approve, anchor confirmed, activate, and the verify results. Next, the MongoDB collections. Users, geofences, documents and document versions are the authoritative data. The anchors collection is derived, because the chain is the real evidence. Verification records and audit logs are history. The ids link them: a version points to its document, an anchor points to its version, and the storage key points to the file. Last, the split. MongoDB holds state, fingerprints and audit. The private bucket holds the file bytes. The Sepolia contract holds the hash, version, event type and time. One honest gap: the audit log has no hash chain yet, so someone with database write access could alter it. That is a planned improvement.
