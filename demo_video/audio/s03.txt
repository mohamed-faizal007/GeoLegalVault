Now the Administrator's Admin Panel, which has Users, Geofences, Reports and System Health. The Geofences tab lists the zones. A zone is a polygon, a shape made of longitude and latitude points. Our demo zone, HQ Campus, is a small box around latitude 11.67 and longitude 78.15. Every request is tested against it on the server. Now the Users tab. We create a throwaway account with the Auditor role. A new user starts at the lowest clearance, Public, and the password field is masked. We will use this account later. Login also has protection. On this throwaway account only, I type wrong passwords on purpose. After five failures the account is locked for a short time, and the next attempt is refused with: Too many attempts. That slows down password guessing.
