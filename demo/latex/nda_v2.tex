\section*{Parties}
This Agreement is made between \textbf{Northwind Legal Associates} (``Disclosing Party'') and
\textbf{Contoso Infrastructure Ltd.} (``Receiving Party''). All names are fictional.

\section{Confidential Information}
``Confidential Information'' means any non-public information disclosed by either party, in any
form, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

\section{Obligations}
The Receiving Party shall (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, (b) not disclose it
to third parties without written consent, and (c) protect it with at least reasonable care.

\section{Term}
This Agreement remains in force for \textbf{three (3) years} from the date of signature.

\section{Governing Law}
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall be referred to arbitration seated in Chennai.
